National Health Insurance Authorization

Nigerian families deserve access to quality and affordable healthcare. The Nigerian Family Health Protection Program provides an opportunity to support parents, children, siblings and extended family members through health insurance.

Supporting health insurance for loved ones is more than providing money for medical bills. It is an investment in family protection, dignity, financial security and longer life.

Our purpose is to promote access to affordable health insurance coverage and help protect families across Nigeria.
